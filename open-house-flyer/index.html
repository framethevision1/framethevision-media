About this home

Set behind a low hedge on one of Oak Meadows' quietest lanes, this light-filled farmhouse opens to a vaulted great room where a wall of glass folds back to the veranda. The chef's kitchen anchors it all around a quartz waterfall island.

Upstairs, the primary suite reads like a private wing — a spa bath with a freestanding soaking tub, a walk-in dressing room, and a reading nook framing the oaks. Owned solar and an EV-ready garage keep it as sensible as it is beautiful.
